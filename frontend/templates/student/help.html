Viewing your Exam Card

Open Exam Card to view your published examination details. Use Print and select your device's “Save as PDF” option if you need a PDF copy.

Finding your examination venue

Open an examination from your timetable and choose Navigate. Building coordinates are used when available; indoor directions may be limited to the saved floor and room details.

Open timetable

Understanding eligibility

Eligibility statuses come from the system's available examination and clearance records. A pending or unavailable result is not the same as being eligible. Contact the Examination Office through official university channels if your status needs review.

Timetable or venue changes

Check your timetable and notifications regularly for current published details. If the displayed information appears incorrect, contact the Examination Office using the university's official contact directory.

Account and privacy

Your academic profile is read-only and linked to your authenticated account. Do not share your password, Exam Card verification link, or personal device access with others.
